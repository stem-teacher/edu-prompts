## Extraction Notes

### Q1 - Structural Formulas
- Four structural formulas showing different arrangements for C₅H₁₀O
- Options A-D with full structural formulas showing all bonds
- Ensure all H atoms, C atoms, and bonds are clearly visible

### Q4 - Mass Spectrum
- X-axis: m/z from 0 to 100
- Y-axis: Relative abundance (0-100%)
- Major peaks labeled: 27, 41, 60 (100%), 73, 88
- Include axis labels

### Q5-Q6 - Titration Curve
- X-axis: Volume of acid added (mL), 0-50
- Y-axis: pH, 0-14
- Curve shows base being titrated with acid
- Sharp drop around 25-27 mL

### Q8 - Ester Structure
- Shows: CH₃CH₂—O—C(=O)—CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₃
- Can be simplified or shown in structural formula format
- Carbonyl (C=O) must be clearly visible

### Q14 - NMR Spectrum
- Single large peak around 1-2 ppm
- Chemical shift range: 0-10 ppm
- Include axis labels

### Q14 - Structural Formulas
- Four C₄H₉Cl isomers:
  - A: (CH₃)₃C-Cl (2-chloro-2-methylpropane)
  - B: (CH₃)₂CH-CH₂Cl (1-chloro-2-methylpropane)
  - C: CH₃CH₂CH(Cl)CH₃ (2-chlorobutane)
  - D: CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂Cl (1-chlorobutane)

### Q20 - Calibration Curve
- X-axis: Concentration of MnO₄⁻ (mg L⁻¹), 0-55
- Y-axis: Absorbance, 0-1.2
- Linear relationship with data points marked
- Include gridlines

### Q23 - Apparatus
- Spirit burner with ethanol at bottom
- Tripod with gauze supporting beaker
- Aluminum beaker containing 105 g water
- Thermometer in the water
- Labels for all components

### Q24 - Conductivity Graph
- X-axis: Volume of barium hydroxide (mL), 0-30
- Y-axis: Conductivity (arbitrary units)
- V-shaped curve with minimum around 17 mL

### Q25 - Concentration Graph
- X-axis: Time (min)
- Y-axis: Concentration (mol L⁻¹)
- Four lines for H₂ (top), H₂O, CO₂, and CO (bottom)
- Vertical line at time T showing CO removal
- Dashed/dotted lines showing new equilibrium after time T

### Q26 - Four Spectra
This is a complex image with four separate spectra:

1. **Mass Spectrum** (top left):
   - Peaks at m/z: 20, 45, 57, 73, 74 (100%)

2. **IR Spectrum** (top right):
   - Wavenumber range: 500-3000 cm⁻¹
   - Broad O-H stretch around 3000 cm⁻¹
   - C=O stretch around 1700 cm⁻¹

3. **¹H NMR** (bottom left):
   - Chemical shift: 0-12 ppm
   - Peaks around 1.00, 2.00-3.00 ppm

4. **¹³C NMR** (bottom right):
   - Chemical shift: 0-200 ppm
   - Two major peaks

### Q32 - Boiling Points Graph
- X-axis: Carbon atoms per molecule (0-7)
- Y-axis: Boiling point (°C), 0-175
- Two series: Alcohols (♦) and Thiols (×)
- Data points for CH₃CH₂OH and CH₃CH₂SH labeled
- Alcohols show higher boiling points throughout

### Q34 - Reaction Scheme
- Shows 3-step synthesis pathway
- Starting material (A): Chloroethane
- Step 1 → Product (B): Ethanol
- Step 2 → Product (C): Ethanoic acid
- Step 3 (combining B and C) → Product (D): Ethyl ethanoate
- All structures shown with full structural formulas
